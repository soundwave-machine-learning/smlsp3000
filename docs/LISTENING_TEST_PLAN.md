# Blind listening and product decision gates

Revision: planning V1 · 2026-10-06

Status: DRAFT PLANNING CONTRACT. Documentation creation authorized; implementation and release not authorized.

## Purpose and prerequisites

CHAIN-EXP-018 is NOT EXECUTED. G-07 product selection depends on human judgment using measurable fitted candidates; G-10 final UI/preset acceptance depends on production/native behavior. It cannot be replaced with an agent listening claim or synthetic blind scores. No listener cohort size, score cutoff, or ABX success criterion exists in research: owner/validation lead freezes protocol and criterion before trials.

## Trial design

Use hardware capture, fitted reference, justified simplified candidate and bypass with hidden reference and randomized blind labels. ABX or MUSHRA-style protocol is chosen and logged, not conflated. CandidateB comparison reference may support reducedC only if validation and listening justify retained/removed blocks. SelectA only if approved product purpose accepts its omitted sample path; do not call it a complete machine model. Lack of MPC audibility is a valid result; do not reward assumed warmth.

Materials: full mix, drums, bass-heavy/sub-heavy, bright mix, dense mastered, dynamic premaster, wide and quiet-tail cases. SP excerpts≤2.3s total compatible with valid hardware captures; short duration is an explicit inference/coverage limit. Listener may replay trials with protocol-defined rules; no unsupported full-song hardware capture. Protect listener level and avoid clipping by calibrated reproducible playback settings within the approved operator protocol.

Listening-only derivatives are RMS matched across each excerpt, logged scalar per condition, outside the model. Fidelity validation keeps1kHz calibration scalar. Blind matched audio never replaces raw captures. Record source excerpt/hash, listening-derivative hash, calibration, scalar, route/params, asset/build version, randomized ordering/seed, hidden reference key in separate result access, playback chain, listener metadata as permitted, trial responses, uncertainty and stated interpretation. Every filename/sidecar is LISTENING STIMULUS, not HARDWARE MEASUREMENT.

## Required owner decisions

After objective fit + trials, record shippingA/B/C, SP default/output variants, MPC main/individual scope, labeled stereo abstraction, controls/mappings/headroom intent, retained mechanisms and claims. Unsupported path, drive macro, tuned/reverse/mid-side/SSM mode requires a new approved scope and evidence gate; none is included by default. S6 cannot begin product optimization before this decision. S7 final listening/visual review checks automation, meters, latency/bypass and preset headroom descriptions; it cannot retroactively remove objective failures.
